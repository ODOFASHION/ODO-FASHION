PRIVACY POLICY.

ODOFASHION (“ODO”, “we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains the basic information we collect and how we use it when you create an account, place an order, or contact us.

Information we collect

We may collect your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, account details, order details, and messages you send to customer support.

How we use it

We use this information to create and manage accounts, process COD orders, deliver products, provide customer support, maintain order history, prevent misuse, and improve ODOFASHION.

Payments

ODOFASHION currently offers Cash on Delivery. Online payment may be introduced later. We do not ask customers to send card or banking passwords through our support channels.

Third parties

Our website may use services such as Supabase for authentication and order data, Google for optional sign-in, GitHub Pages for hosting, and WhatsApp for customer support.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect account and order information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may contact ODO customer support about your account or order information. We may retain records needed for legitimate business, legal, fraud-prevention, and order-history purposes.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact ODOFASHION through the customer-support options on the website.
