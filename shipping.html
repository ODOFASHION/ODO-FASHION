SHIPPING & DELIVERY.

Delivery area

ODOFASHION offers Cash on Delivery across Nepal.

Delivery timing

Delivery time can vary by location, courier availability, weather, public holidays, and order volume. We will use the phone number provided at checkout for delivery communication when needed.

Address accuracy

Customers are responsible for providing a correct phone number and delivery address. Incorrect or incomplete information can delay delivery or result in a failed delivery.

Delivery charges

Any applicable delivery charge will be shown or communicated before dispatch. Current promotions may include different delivery terms.

Failed delivery

If a delivery cannot be completed, ODO may contact the customer to arrange another attempt. Repeated failed deliveries may affect future COD eligibility.
