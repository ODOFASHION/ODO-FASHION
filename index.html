03 / ABOUT ODO

BUILT TO
SEE BEYOND.

ODO Fashion is a premium + streetwear expression of a larger idea.

ODO is not only clothing. It is identity, vision and a way of seeing the world beyond the ordinary.

Built from Nepal with a global ambition, ODO combines strong typography, meaningful words and modern streetwear silhouettes. The owl represents wisdom, awareness and the ability to see what others may overlook.

ODO Fashion is the first fashion expression under GREATODOUNIVERSE — a future parent company imagined to grow into a universe of many ideas and companies.

For now, ODO is at the beginning. The first designs are being created with care, while the next chapter is being prepared.
