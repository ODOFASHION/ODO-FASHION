RETURNS & EXCHANGE.

Before requesting an exchange

Please check your size and product details before placing an order. Because ODOFASHION currently operates primarily through COD, customers should inspect the product and contact support promptly if there is a genuine issue.

Eligible issues

We may consider exchange requests for an incorrect item, manufacturing defect, or significant damage that occurred before delivery.

Size exchange

Size exchanges depend on available stock and may be subject to the condition that the item is unworn, unwashed, and in resalable condition.

How to request

Contact ODO customer support with your order number, product details, and clear photos when relevant. We will review the request and provide next steps.

Non-returnable conditions

Items that are worn, washed, altered, damaged after delivery, or missing required tags/packaging may not qualify for exchange.
