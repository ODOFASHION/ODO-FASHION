ODOFASHION / REFUNDS

REFUND POLICY.

ODOFASHION currently offers Cash on Delivery. Online payment is coming soon, so refunds for online payments will be handled under the payment method and terms displayed when that service launches.

COD orders

For COD orders, a refund normally does not apply because payment is collected on delivery. Eligible product issues may instead be handled through an approved exchange, replacement, or other resolution communicated by ODO support.

Future online payments

When online payment becomes available, the website will provide additional payment and refund information before purchase. Refund timing may depend on the payment provider and the customer's bank or wallet.

Contact

For an order issue, contact ODO customer support with your order number and relevant details.
